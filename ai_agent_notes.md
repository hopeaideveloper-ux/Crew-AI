- **Definition of AI Agents**: AI agents are computer programs that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. They can operate autonomously or assist humans in various tasks.

- **Types of AI Agents**: There are different types of AI agents, including reactive agents (which respond to current situations), deliberative agents (which plan actions based on goals), and learning agents (which improve their performance over time through experience).

- **Applications of AI Agents**: AI agents are used in various fields such as customer service (chatbots), healthcare (diagnostic tools), finance (trading algorithms), and robotics (autonomous vehicles), showcasing their versatility and impact on everyday life.
